# Pentair Fit Gap Assessment – demo script (about 10 minutes)

## Before you start
1. Download `Pentair-Process-Fit-Assessment.html` to the laptop (Desktop is fine). Do **not** open it from a Teams/Outlook preview or a browser download bar preview.
2. Double-click it. It must open in **Chrome or Edge**. No internet, login or install is needed.
3. Click **Demo guide** (top right). The 8 steps below are in the panel; each step opens the right screen.
4. **If anything goes wrong, go to the Home screen and click Reset demo data.** It restores Plant A and Plant B. Your own assessments are kept.

## The 8 steps
| # | Screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home** | "One workspace for every fit gap assessment: owner, progress and fit score. Plant A is finished (our reference); Plant B is in progress." |
| 2 | **Start a new assessment** | "Name it, pick the process template (Pentair's 399-process master list), and start blank or pre-filled." Type a name, click **Start assessment**. |
| 3 | **Procure to Pay sections, Mark all Fit** | "Work one area at a time. **Mark all Fit** rates a whole section in one click, with a confirmation and an undo." |
| 4 | **Fit in one click; Partial Fit with comment + transcript** | "Fit is one click. Partial Fit and Not Fit need a reason." Click **Partial Fit**, type a comment, **Add transcript → Insert sample transcript**, **Save**. |
| 5 | **Generate BRD, Copy, Download** | "BRDs here are sample output. Going forward they're generated from Pentair's past rollout documents and the meeting transcript." Click **Generate BRD**, pick **PTP-023**, then **Copy text** and **Download (.docx)**. Six processes have full sample BRDs: PTP-005, 017, 018, 023, 033, 050. |
| 6 | **Mark complete below 100%** | "The consultant can close an assessment before every process is rated. Not Applicable counts toward completion, not toward the score." Click **Mark complete**. |
| 7 | **Summary and drill-down** | "Fit, Partial Fit and Not Fit are shares of **scored** processes. Not Applicable is shown separately as a share of **rated** processes." Open a track, then click an area to jump back to its processes. |
| 8 | **Plant A reference** | "Plant A is the previous rollout, with comments and a transcript on PTP-023. A new plant can start from it, so we only discuss the differences." |

## Questions you may get
- **Security / where does the data go?** "It runs entirely in the browser. No data leaves the machine. Production runs on our servers."
- **Where is my data stored?** In this browser on this laptop only. If the browser blocks storage, the header shows "Demo mode – changes are kept while this tab is open."

## What is sample today vs. the roadmap
| Sample / demo today | Roadmap |
|---|---|
| Plant A and Plant B ratings and comments | Real assessments from Pentair's rollouts |
| BRD content (six hand-written samples; other processes use the generic template) | **LLM-generated BRD / FSD / TSD** from past rollout documents and the meeting transcript |
| Transcripts ("Insert sample transcript") | Real meeting transcripts (upload .txt, .vtt, .docx) |
| Process list only (L1–L4 plus detail) | **L5 and custom-object inventory** linked to each process |
| Storage in the browser on one laptop | **SharePoint storage** and shared access for the team |
